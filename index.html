How it plays (short version)

  • Bet 5, 10, 20, 40 or 50 casino coins to enter a round, or spectate until the next one. Everyone starts with 1000 coins; broke players rebuy 1000 free coins between rounds.
  • Hit, stay, or double down (2 cards only). Get 5 cards without busting and you beat even a dealer 21 — 5 cards totalling exactly 21 is the strongest hand there is.
  • Special cards: 6 in the deck. Every time one is dealt, a die (0, 0, 1, 1, 2, 2) is rolled and the result is added to a shared total — every special card on the table is worth that total. Specials are always face-up.
  • Battle Bonyas: a rare secret round (12%) where the single best winning hand takes a huge bonus — up to 1200 coins on a 50-coin doubled-down bet.
  • The dealer stands on 17 (including soft 17). The full rules are under 📖 Rules.
  • 🎵 The main song loops every match; the battle song plays only during a Battle Bonyas round.
